# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

At **QuickEMS**, we want this project to be a welcoming and respectful space for everyone.

Whether you're fixing a typo, adding a feature, or suggesting improvements — you should feel comfortable contributing without fear of judgment or harassment.

---

## Expected Behavior

If you're contributing here, keep it simple:

* Be respectful in discussions and feedback
* Accept constructive criticism without getting defensive
* Focus on improving the project, not proving a point
* Help others when you can
* Communicate clearly and honestly

Basically: act like a decent human being.

---

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following won’t be tolerated:

* Harassment, hate speech, or personal attacks
* Disrespectful or dismissive comments
* Trolling or intentionally disruptive behavior
* Sharing someone’s private information without permission
* Any form of discrimination

If it makes the space uncomfortable or hostile, it doesn’t belong here.

---

## Code Review Etiquette

A lot of conflict happens here, so let’s keep it healthy:

* Critique the code, not the person
* Explain *why* something should change
* Suggest improvements instead of just pointing out issues
* Be open to discussion — there’s rarely only one “correct” way

---

## Responsibility of Maintainers

Maintainers are expected to:

* Enforce this Code of Conduct fairly
* Review contributions respectfully
* Provide clear and helpful feedback
* Take action if rules are violated

---

## Reporting Issues

If you face or notice unacceptable behavior:

* Open an issue (if appropriate), or
* Contact the maintainer directly

Provide as much context as possible so the situation can be handled properly.

---

## Enforcement

Violations may lead to:

* Warning
* Removal from discussions
* Rejection of contributions
* Ban from the project (in serious cases)

---

## Final Note

QuickEMS is a project built to solve real problems.
A healthy community makes it stronger.

Respect people. Write good code. Keep things constructive.
